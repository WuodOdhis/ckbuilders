# Week 12: CKBuilders Nairobi Meetup

**Dates:** July 19, 2026

This week was community-focused. I joined a CKBuilders meetup in Nairobi where we
introduced CKB to other builders in Kenya and connected with people working on
different projects and products.

Neon, the lead of CKBuilders, also visited Kenya and joined the meetup. It was a
good moment for the local builder community to connect directly with the wider
CKBuilders program.

## Nairobi Meetup

The meetup was useful because it moved CKB from online learning into a local room
with other builders. We introduced CKB, talked about the ecosystem, and spent
time with people building in Kenya.

It was also fun. That matters. Meeting builders in person made the program feel
more practical and local, not just remote learning or isolated development.

## Project Showcases

Other CKBuilders showcased their projects and products. That was one of the most
valuable parts of the meetup because it showed that people are not only learning
theory. They are trying to turn ideas into usable things.

The session created space for builders to see each other's work, ask questions,
and understand where CKB might fit into future products or experiments.

## Why This Matters

The biggest lesson from the meetup is that ecosystem growth also depends on
local builder awareness. A strong technical ecosystem needs people who understand
what the chain is for, what makes it different, and what kinds of products can be
built on top of it.

Introducing CKB to other Kenyan builders matters because it expands the group of
people who can experiment with the Cell Model, CKB applications, and future local
use cases.

## Current Status

No new major code milestone was completed this week. The value of the week was
community connection and ecosystem exposure.

The practical outcomes were:

- CKB was introduced to more builders in Kenya;
- other CKBuilders got to showcase what they are building;
- Neon's visit helped connect the local meetup to the wider CKBuilders program;
- the meetup created more energy around building and sharing CKB-related work.

## Takeaway

The Nairobi meetup showed that CKBuilders is not only about individual progress.
It is also about growing a local builder network. The next step is to keep
building while also helping more developers in Kenya understand CKB and see where
they can contribute.
