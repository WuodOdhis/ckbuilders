# Day 1: CKBuilders Nairobi Meetup

**Date:** July 19, 2026

## Objective

Take part in a local CKBuilders meetup in Nairobi and help introduce CKB to more
builders in Kenya. The goal was not a deep technical work session. It was to meet
other builders, share what CKB is about, and see what people in the local builder
community are creating.

## Nairobi Meetup

We had a CKBuilders meetup in Nairobi with other builders from Kenya. Neon, the
lead of CKBuilders, also visited Kenya and joined the session.

The meetup was useful because it brought CKB into a local builder setting. We
introduced CKB to other builders, talked about why the ecosystem is interesting,
and connected with people who are already building products in Kenya.

## What Happened

The session was more about ecosystem introduction and project sharing than
technical debugging.

- We introduced CKB to builders who are active in the Kenyan ecosystem.
- Other CKBuilders showcased their projects and products.
- We got to see how different builders are thinking about applications,
  infrastructure, and user-facing products.
- The visit from Neon made the meetup feel connected to the wider CKBuilders
  program, not just a local side event.

## Takeaway

The meetup was fun and useful. It showed that there is real value in bringing CKB
into local builder communities and letting people see what the ecosystem is about
through actual projects and products.

The main takeaway is that community exposure matters. Technical progress is one
side of the work, but helping other builders discover CKB and understand what can
be built on it is also important.
